Walking access to essential services

Access score

0–100 per neighborhood. For every home, we measure the walk along streets and paths to the nearest service of each kind. A walk of up to 400 m (5 min) earns 100 points, up to 800 m 60, up to 1,600 m (20 min) 30, farther 0. A neighborhood's score for a service is the average over its residents (homes with more residents count more); the overall score combines the five services with the weights below.
